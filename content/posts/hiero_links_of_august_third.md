## Hiero Links For The Week Of August 3rd

This week featured a lot of new and exciting updates within the Hiero Organization. 
Below are links to some of the things that happened this past week. **Check it out!**

---
**[Angelina C](https://github.com/aceppaluni)** added [coderabbit planning](https://github.com/hiero-ledger/hiero-website/pull/540) to the Hiero Website to assist developers in working on issues.

**[Phillip N](https://github.com/phillip-nyinomujuni)** migrated deprecated [set_amount_in_tinybars()](https://github.com/hiero-ledger/hiero-sdk-python/pull/2501) calls to set_hbar_amount(Hbar.from_tinybars(...)) in the Python SDK's TCK handler and consensus example, eliminating deprecation warnings while preserving behavior and keeping deprecated test coverage intact.

**[Boris B](https://github.com/boris-bonin)** updated the [documentation](https://github.com/hiero-ledger/solo/pull/5613) for the Hiero Solo repository to maintain up-to-date information for the Solo CLI.

**[Andrew B](https://github.com/andrewb1269)** updated the [pinned solo](https://github.com/hiero-ledger/hiero-sdk-java/pull/2868) version used in the CI across three jobs in the Java SDK.
