## Hiero Links For The Week Of August 31st

This week featured a lot of new and exciting updates within the Hiero Organization. 
Below are links to some of the things that happened this past week. **Check it out!**

---
**[Manish D](https://github.com/manishdait)** added support for [opt-in recipt/record node failover](https://github.com/hiero-ledger/hiero-sdk-python/pull/2527) to the Python SDK.

**[Jessica G](https://github.com/jwagantall)** created a blog post for the [TSC nominations](https://github.com/hiero-ledger/hiero-website/pull/606) in the Hiero Website.

**[Noah T](https://github.com/Ndacyayisenga-droid)** added the [wipeNFT and WipeNFT’s](https://github.com/hiero-ledger/hiero-enterprise-java/pull/297) to the NFTClient in the Enterprise-Java repository.

**[Ivan I](https://github.com/ivannov)** fixed the [API suite tests](https://github.com/hiero-ledger/hiero-block-node/pull/3592) that were failing when duplicate blocks sent an acknowledgement in the response in Hiero Block Node.
