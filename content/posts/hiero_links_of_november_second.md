## Hiero Links For The Week Of November 2nd

This week featured a lot of new and exciting updates within the Hiero Organization. 
Below are links to some of the things that happened this past week. **Check it out!**

---
**[Anchit G](https://github.com/anchit-goel)** added the [getMirrorNodeTokenBalance](https://github.com/hiero-ledger/hiero-sdk-python/pull/2764) TCK moethod to the Python SDK.

**[Manish D](https://github.com/manishdait)** overwrote the [guava dependency](https://github.com/hiero-ledger/hiero-enterprise-java/pull/417) to 33.7.2 in the Enterprise Java Repository. 

**[Jessie ](https://github.com/Jexsie)** fixed treating a [complete DER private key](https://github.com/hiero-ledger/hiero-sdk-js/pull/4651) as DER in isDerKey in the JavaScript SDK. 

**[Michael H](https://github.com/netopyr)** switched [consensus benchmarks](https://github.com/hiero-ledger/hiero-consensus-node/pull/28087) to 28 nodes in the Consensus Node Repository.
