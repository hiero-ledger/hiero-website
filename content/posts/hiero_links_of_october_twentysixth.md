## Hiero Links For The Week Of October 26th

This week featured a lot of new and exciting updates within the Hiero Organization. 
Below are links to some of the things that happened this past week. **Check it out!**

---
**[Noah T](https://github.com/Ndacyayisenga-droid)** added the [-SNAPSHOT qualifier](https://github.com/hiero-ledger/hiero-enterprise-java/pull/400) for all modules for the next 0.23.0 version of Enterprise Java.

**[Angelina C](https://github.com/aceppaluni)** posted a new blog post for the links of [October 19th](https://hiero.org/blog/hiero-links-of-october-nineteenth) on the Website.

**[Michael H](https://github.com/netopyr)** replaced [for-each loops](https://github.com/hiero-ledger/hiero-consensus-node/pull/27932) to improve performance in the Consensus Node.

**[Stoyan D](https://github.com/sdimitrov9)** fixed [treasury change migration](https://github.com/hiero-ledger/hiero-mirror-node/pull/14553) exceeding the JDBC parameter limit in Mirror Node.
