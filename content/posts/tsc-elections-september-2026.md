## 📣📣📣  Calling all Hiero Community!!!  📣📣📣
## 2026 September TSC Nominations are now open! 

All current contributors for the [Hiero Project](https://github.com/hiero-ledger) are welcomed to post their nominations for our upcoming TSC's Maintainer Seat elections.
Participants are welcomed to nominate themselves or nominate others with their consent. 

Three positions will be open for elections:

- 2 Maintainer Seats
- 1 TSC Voted Seat

## 📅 TSC Election Timeline for September 2026

**August 25 – September 8: Nominations**
All candidates submit their nominations for a Maintainer TSC Seat.

**September 9 – September 20: First Ballot**
The first ballot is open for Hiero maintainers to vote.
Maintainers vote to select two candidates for TSC seats.

**September 21 – October 2: Second Ballot**
The second ballot is open for the current Hiero TSC to vote.
The TSC selects the third candidate from the candidates remaining after the first ballot.

**October 6: Election Results**
The results of both ballots are announced at the [TSC meeting](https://zoom-lfx.platform.linuxfoundation.org/meeting/95775743341?password=c07443bf-b0e6-4a68-93f1-5c7ce9bb49ab), establishing the three newly selected TSC members.
The newly formed TSC will collectively select its Chair and Co-Chair.

## 📝 Who is a Good Candidate for Nominations

A nominee is someone who is able to:

- Cover deep technical domains from the Hiero project and understand its composition.
- Have the available bandwidth to make the time to invest in the TSC responsibilities. 
- Demonstrate an advanced level of professional experience as engineers in the scope of Hiero
- Operate neutrally in discussions and put the goals and success of Hiero in balance with any particular sub-project governed by the TSC.

Candidates can nominate themselves or be nominated by someone else with their consent.

Our [documentation](https://github.com/hiero-ledger/governance/tree/main/elections/nominees/sep-2026-election#how-to-nominate-a-candidate) describes the process for filing a nomination. 

## 🗳️ How Will the Vote Work

There will be two ballots created:

- **Ballot 1** - All Hiero Maintainers will be eligible to select two TSC Maintainer Seat candidates to replace the previous ones.
- **Ballot 2** - The current TSC members will be eligible to select the third TSC Voted Seat member from the pool or remaining candidates from the first ballot.

Helios sends notifications to all eligible voters via your configured GitHub Notifications.

At the same time, the links to the ballots will be made available once the vote becomes open. 

As a reminder, please locate the "Log In" button in the lower left side of the screen and use your GitHub ID to register.

## 📣 Announcing the winner

The results for each Maintainer seat and the TSC Voted seat will be reviewed by the LFDT Community Architects and formally announced during the [TSC meeting happening
on Oct 06](https://zoom-lfx.platform.linuxfoundation.org/meeting/95775743341?password=c07443bf-b0e6-4a68-93f1-5c7ce9bb49ab).

## ✅ Questions

For any questions regarding the voting process, we encourage community members to attend the next [Hiero TSC Call](https://zoom-lfx.platform.linuxfoundation.org/meetings/hiero?view=week).

Additionally, members are encouraged to explore the [Helios Voting](https://vote.heliosvoting.org/) platform to become familiar with its features and functionality.

Find more information in our [official documentation](https://github.com/hiero-ledger/governance/tree/main/elections/nominees/sep-2026-election).
